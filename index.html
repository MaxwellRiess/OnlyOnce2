One Word

Draw a secret word, or show your one-word clue to the table.

Difficulty
3

Turn the screen away from the guesser

No spaces. Matching clues cancel each other out, so be original.

How to play

A word game you play together, not against each other.

You need three or more players, each with this app open on their own phone.

One player guesses a secret word. Everyone else gives them a clue, but each clue can only be one word.

The catch: if two players write the same clue, both are cancelled.

Each round

  1. Choose a guesser. They close their eyes.
  2. Someone taps Get a word. Turn the screen away from the guesser during the countdown, then show the word to everyone else.
  3. Each clue-giver taps Give a clue and types one word. Keep it to yourself for now.
  4. While the guesser’s eyes are still closed, the clue-givers compare clues. Cancel any that match.
  5. The guesser opens their eyes and sees the clues that are left. They get one guess, or they can pass.

Then someone else becomes the guesser.

Clues

What counts as a match

The same word, or a close version of it. Plurals and words from the same family both count.

  • DogDogs
  • PrincePrincess
  • BakeBaker

Obvious clues are the riskiest. The best ones help the guesser and surprise everyone else.

Not allowed

  • The secret word, or part of it
  • The secret word in another language
  • A word that sounds the same, like Knight for Night
  • A made-up word

Numbers, abbreviations and sounds are fine: 007, NASA, Shh.

Difficulty

Easy
Everyday things you could point at.
Apple, Bridge, Penguin
Medium
Less common things, or things with close look-alikes.
Kayak, Cathedral, Platypus
Hard
Abstract ideas and concepts. You can’t point at them, so clues have to work harder.
Nostalgia, Gravity, Karma

Any draws from all three.
